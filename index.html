Editorial Statement

as flowers is a meeting point of incisive writing and artful design. A magazine where bold ideas take root and unfurl af(as flowers).

Clean lines, deliberate color, and a quiet thread of floral imagery shape our visual identity. We value clarity, resonance, and work that lingers. A home for bold voices.
